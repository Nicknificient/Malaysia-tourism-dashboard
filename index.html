What This Reveals

What the data tells us about Malaysia's visitors in 2025

🌏
ASEAN remains the backbone
ASEAN dominates the treemap with the largest share of arrivals. Singapore alone contributes 9.3M visitors — nearly 1 in 4 tourists — reflecting Malaysia's deep regional connectivity.
📈
Recovery is uneven across markets
China and India have surpassed their 2019 benchmarks, while Japan and South Korea still trail. Emerging markets like Bangladesh and Myanmar are growing fastest of all.
🇰🇷
South Korea shows the largest gap
Its 2019 benchmark band stretches to 673K but 2025 arrivals sit at only 463K — the widest shortfall visible across both bullet charts, suggesting structural shifts in Korean outbound travel that have yet to reverse.

04 · Occupancy Patterns

When and Where Are Hotels Busiest?

Monthly occupancy patterns reveal seasonal pressure and state-level differences in hotel demand.

Year-End Occupancy Peaks Across Several States

Darker teal = higher occupancy · States sorted by full-year average

🔍 How to Read the Pattern

🌧️
East coast peaks during holiday months
Terengganu drops from 53% in Oct to 35% in Dec; Kelantan from 47% to 30%. Meanwhile Pulau Pinang, Selangor and Johor stay stable or darken — a sharp east–west divide driven by the Northeast Monsoon (Nov–Jan).
🎄
West coast demand stays more stable
Johor, Pulau Pinang and Putrajaya all show noticeably darker December cells versus November — year-end holidays and school breaks drive a broad west-coast rebound just as the east coast reaches its seasonal low.

Fragile by Nature, Resilient by Demand
